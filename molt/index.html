BUILT AROUND THE DRIVE

More than a pin on a map.

Molt combines everyday map navigation with shared-trip tools for convoys, friends, families and anyone who wants to know where the others are without constant phone calls or messages.

➤

Meet & Drive

Create a Molt, invite the other drivers and see everyone moving on the same live map while heading toward a shared destination.

⌖

Smart Meetups

Molt can support sensible meetup points along the route so separate drivers can merge into a convoy without unnecessary backtracking.

◉

Share My Drive

Share one trip with someone who is not travelling. They can follow your progress and arrival information without joining as a driver.

▣

Browser Watch

A shared Molt link opens in a browser, making it possible to follow the active drive without installing the app.

⚠

Community Reports

Road hazards, roadworks, crashes, police and other useful driving reports can appear directly on the live map.

∞

Everyday Navigation

Molt is designed to work as a normal map and navigation app too. Shared driving modes are extra layers, not a requirement.

TWO WAYS TO SHARE A DRIVE

Travel together — or just let them know you're coming.

MEET & DRIVE

Everyone travelling is part of the Molt.

The host creates the drive and shares a code or link. Participants join, see the destination, each other's live position and ETA, and can stay coordinated as the trip changes.

MULTI-DRIVER
SHARE MY DRIVE

One traveller. One simple live link.

Send a trip-scoped link to the person waiting for you. They can watch your drive, updated ETA and arrival progress without becoming a travelling participant.

VIEWER DOESN'T NEED THE APP
MOLT WATCH

Follow the drive from a browser.

Every shared Molt can have a browser-friendly Watch link. The page is being structured to accept an individual session ID and, once connected to Molt's live data, display the active destination, drivers, ETAs, meetup status and trip state.
