BUILT AROUND THE DRIVE

More than a pin on a map.

Molt combines everyday navigation with shared-trip tools for convoys, friends, families and anyone who wants to know where the others are without constant phone calls or messages.

➤

Meet & Drive

Create a Molt, invite the other drivers and see everyone moving on the same live map while heading toward a shared destination.

⌖

Smart Meetups

Molt can support sensible meetup points along the route so separate drivers can merge into a convoy without unnecessary backtracking.

◉

Share My Drive

Share one trip with someone who is not travelling. They can follow your progress and arrival information without joining as a driver.

▣

Browser Watch

A shared Molt link opens in a browser, making it possible to follow the active drive without installing the app.

⚠

Community Reports

Road hazards, roadworks, crashes, police and other useful driving reports can appear directly on the live map.

∞

Everyday Navigation

Molt is designed to work as a normal map and navigation app too. Shared driving modes are extra layers, not a requirement.
